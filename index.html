COMMON QUESTIONS

Straight answers before we get started.

A few of the things business owners usually want to know before working with Veridian.

Do I need to sign a long-term contract?

No. Veridian is built around clear, flexible working relationships rather than locking businesses into unnecessary long-term commitments.

Who owns my website?

Your website is built for your business. Any ownership, hosting and ongoing-care arrangements are set out clearly before work starts so you know exactly what is included.

Do you work with businesses across the UK?

Yes. Veridian works with business owners across the UK. The website, lead systems, advertising and follow-up process can all be managed remotely.

How quickly can a new website launch?

Timescales depend on the size of the project, content and any integrations required. Once we understand the scope, we will give you a clear launch plan rather than promise an unrealistic deadline.

How much should I spend on advertising?

There is no sensible one-size-fits-all figure. The right budget depends on your service, location, margins, competition and goals. We can recommend a starting point after reviewing your business.

Can you improve an existing website or system?

Yes. You do not always need to start again. We can look at your current website, lead capture, follow-up and marketing and focus on the areas with the strongest opportunity for improvement.

Privacy Policy

How Veridian Collective handles personal information submitted through our website and services.

Last updated: 5 October 2026

1. Who we are

Veridian Collective provides website design, lead systems and digital marketing services to businesses in the United Kingdom. For privacy enquiries, contact us at rjones@veridiancollective.co.uk.

2. Information we may collect

We may collect information you provide directly to us, such as your name, business name, email address, telephone number and the contents of enquiries you submit. We may also collect limited technical and usage information through website analytics and similar technologies where permitted.

3. How we use your information

We use personal information to respond to enquiries, provide and manage our services, communicate with clients, maintain and improve our website and services, keep appropriate business records, and meet legal or regulatory obligations.

4. Our lawful bases

Depending on the circumstances, we may process personal information because it is necessary to take steps at your request before entering into a contract, to perform a contract, because we have a legitimate business interest that does not override your rights, because we have your consent, or because processing is required by law.

5. Service providers

We may use trusted service providers to operate our website and business, such as website hosting, analytics, customer relationship management, communications and payment providers. They may process personal information only as needed to provide their services to us and subject to appropriate safeguards.

6. International transfers

Some service providers may process information outside the United Kingdom. Where this happens, we take reasonable steps to ensure an appropriate legal safeguard is used where required by UK data protection law.

7. How long we keep information

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including providing services, handling enquiries, maintaining business records and meeting legal, accounting or reporting requirements.

8. Cookies and analytics

Our website may use essential technologies required for the site to operate. Where we use non-essential analytics, advertising or similar cookies, we will seek consent where required. You can change or withdraw applicable cookie choices through the controls provided on the website.

9. Your rights

Under UK data protection law, depending on the circumstances, you may have rights to access, correct or erase your personal information, restrict or object to certain processing, request transfer of information, and withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

10. Complaints

If you have a privacy concern, please contact us first so we can try to resolve it. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services or legal obligations change. The latest version will be published on this page with the updated date shown above.
